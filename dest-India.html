Best Time to Visit

A Country of Many Seasons

India spans deserts, coasts and mountains — so the "right" season depends on where you're headed. Here's a quick guide.

Oct – Mar
Winter
Crisp, sunny days — ideal for Rajasthan, Delhi and the plains.
Apr – Jun
Summer
Hot on the plains, but perfect timing for Himalayan treks.
Jul – Sep
Monsoon
Lush greenery — beautiful for Kerala, Goa and the Western Ghats.
Sept – Oct
Post-Monsoon
Fresh air and festival season — Diwali, Durga Puja and more.
